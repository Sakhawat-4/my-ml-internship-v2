# How to Add My Next Case Study

## 📁 Where New Case Studies Go

**Repository:** `https://github.com/Sakhawat-4/my-ml-internship-v2`

**File Path:** `/case-studies/[project-name]/README.md`

**Example:** `/case-studies/customer-churn-prediction/README.md`

---

## 🔄 The 3-Step Process (3-Beat Shape)

### Beat 1: THE PROBLEM
**What was the challenge?**

Write **2-3 sentences** about the business or technical problem.

Answer: *"Why did this project exist?"*

**Example:**
> *"A telecom company was losing 15% of customers monthly. They needed to predict which customers would leave so they could take action before they left."*

---

### Beat 2: WHAT I DID
**How did I solve it?**

- **Tools used:** Python, pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, etc.
- **Approach:** Data cleaning, feature engineering, model selection
- **Key steps:** Bulleted list of main actions

**Example:**
> *"I used Python to clean customer data, engineered 15 new features, and tested 3 ML models including XGBoost. I optimized for recall to catch more churners."*

---

### Beat 3: WHAT CAME OF IT
**What were the results?**

- **Metrics:** Accuracy, F1-score, business impact
- **Lessons learned:** What worked? What didn't?
- **Demo link:** If you have one

**Example:**
> *"Achieved 86% recall and 82% accuracy. Reduced churn by 8% in pilot group, saving $200K annually in customer acquisition costs."*

---
